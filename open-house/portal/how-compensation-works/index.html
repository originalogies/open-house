How compensation works

Four questions decide what is owed to Synergy Realty, LLC on a lead sourced through a George & Noonan open house or Broker introduction.

Step 1How was the lead sourced?

25%

Open house lead

A buyer or seller you meet at a George & Noonan open house.

50%

Broker-provided lead

Troy George or Lucy Noonan introduces the lead to you, made or confirmed in writing.

Fits both categories? The higher rate applies. Percentages are of the gross commission on your side.

Step 2Is it a Qualifying Transaction?

$2,000 or more

Gross commission on your side, before the fee. Referral fee applies.

Under $2,000

Doesn't count toward the limits in Step 3. No referral fee.

Step 3Which side of the transaction?

Seller or landlord side

Fee on the first

The first Qualifying Transaction as a seller or landlord.

Buyer or tenant side

Fee on the first

The first Qualifying Transaction as a buyer or tenant.

Every later transaction

No fee

Later transactions on a side that already had its first.

Buyer purchases the G&N listing itself? No referral fee on that purchase, and it counts as the buyer-side transaction.

Step 4How the commission is divided

Example: the fee comes off the top of a $30,000 gross side commission, then an 80/20 split.

Document it

Lead Report within 24 hours. An undocumented lead still owes the fee.

No expiration

While at Synergy, owed until each side's first Qualifying Transaction.

After departure

Owed on contracts signed within 24 months, paid by the new broker.

Exceptions

Request in writing 2 business days before closing. Broker's discretion.
